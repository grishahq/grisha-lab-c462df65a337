READ THE SMALL MODEL

The math is part of the product.

1. Bound the retries

With retry chance p and at most r retries, expected attempts are 1 + p + p² + … + pʳ. The ceiling is 1 + r. A retry chance of 100% is handled directly.

2. Keep caching explicit

Per attempt, cached input is reusable context × cache hit share. All fresh input and the remaining reusable context use the uncached rate. The estimate applies that share to every attempt, including the first.

3. Know what is missing

Every step runs every time. Tokens per attempt and retry probability stay constant. Tool fees, image or audio generation, hidden reasoning tokens, branching, failed-request billing rules and taxes are not modeled. Add billable reasoning tokens to output yourself if applicable.

Show the full cost equation

runs × Σ [expected attempts × (uncached tokens × input price + cached tokens × cached price + output tokens × output price) / 1,000,000]

Each attempt has the same entered token volume. Retries do not accumulate conversation history in this model. Rates are user inputs and are never fetched or verified. Correlated failures, provider caching thresholds, prefix rules, expiry and rate limits may make real results differ.
